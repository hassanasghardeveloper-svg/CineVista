Autumn 2026 is packed with new releases: superhero horror, a video-game adaptation, survival thrillers and a few big titles still on the way. Here is a quick guide to the newest Hollywood movies of October and November 2026 and what each one is about, so you can pick your next watch.

## Out Now

### 1. [Clayface](/watch/movie/1400940-clayface-2026) (2026)

**Movie · TMDB rating 7.6/10**

A DC horror story about Matt Hagen, a Gotham street kid turned movie star whose face is destroyed by a crime boss. The experimental treatment that fixes it comes at a monstrous price.

### 2. [Street Fighter](/watch/movie/1153576-street-fighter-2026) (2026)

**Movie · TMDB rating 6.4/10**

Set in 1993, this live-action take on the fighting game reunites Ryu and Ken when Chun-Li pulls them into the World Warrior Tournament, and the conspiracy hiding behind it.

### 3. [Fall 2: Deadpoint](/watch/movie/1101412-fall-2-deadpoint-2026) (2026)

**Movie · TMDB rating 7.3/10**

The sequel to the 2022 tower thriller strands two climbers high on a Thai mountain face. If you have a fear of heights, consider yourself warned.

### 4. [Matchbox the Movie](/watch/movie/1148365-matchbox-the-movie-2026) (2026)

**Movie · TMDB rating 7.7/10**

Old childhood friends reunite and accidentally stumble into an international plot. An action-comedy based on the classic toy cars.

### 5. [Masterplan](/watch/movie/1561726-masterplan-2026) (2026)

**Movie · TMDB rating 7.0/10**

A legendary thief plans to steal the Mona Lisa with two young strangers, who turn out to be his long-lost children. A heist comedy with a family twist.

### 6. [Resident Evil](/watch/movie/1423191-resident-evil-2026) (2026)

**Movie · TMDB rating 7.3/10**

A fresh take on the survival-horror franchise: a medical courier gets caught in one terrifying night as everything around him falls apart.

### 7. [Whalefall](/watch/movie/1255833-whalefall-2026) (2026)

**Movie · TMDB rating 6.6/10**

A diver searching for his late father off the California coast is swallowed by a sperm whale and has one hour of oxygen to escape. As strange and tense as it sounds.

### 8. [Hope](/watch/movie/1058424-hope-2026) (2026)

**Movie · TMDB rating 6.8/10**

A Korean creature thriller: police in the remote village of Hope Harbor investigate something attacking the town, and nothing is what it first appears.
