Turkish series (dizi) have fans all over the world, especially in Pakistan and India. Some are huge Ottoman epics, others are family romances or crime stories. This list groups the most loved Turkish series by type, so you can find the right one to start.

## Historical Epics

### 1. [Kuruluş Osman](/watch/tv/95603-kurulus-osman) (2019)

**Series · TMDB rating 8.0/10**

The follow-up to Dirilis Ertugrul: Osman Bey, son of Ertugrul, turns a small tribe into the state that becomes the Ottoman Empire, fighting Byzantines, Mongols and traitors along the way.

### 2. [The Magnificent Century](/watch/tv/34899-the-magnificent-century) (2011)

**Series · TMDB rating 8.1/10**

The reign of Sultan Suleyman, who wanted an empire greater than Alexander's, and the palace intrigue around him over 46 years.

### 3. [Mehmed: Sultan of Conquests](/watch/tv/246621-mehmed-sultan-of-conquests) (2024)

**Series · TMDB rating 8.5/10**

Mehmed II rises from a doubted heir to the young sultan who sets out to conquer Constantinople.

## Romance and Family Drama

### 4. [Yalı Çapkını](/watch/tv/210865-yal-capkn) (2022)

**Series · TMDB rating 8.4/10**

Istanbul playboy Ferit is set up to marry a girl from Gaziantep, but falls for her younger sister Seyran instead, and both families are turned upside down.

### 5. [Far Away](/watch/tv/274556-far-away) (2024)

**Series · TMDB rating 8.5/10**

Widow Alya brings her husband's body and her young son to Mardin to honour his last wish, and finds herself trapped by the powerful Albora tribe.

### 6. [This Sea Will Overflow](/watch/tv/302063-this-sea-will-overflow) (2025)

**Series · TMDB rating 8.3/10**

Set among fishermen used to storms, this is the story of a strong woman and a man who will fight anyone for love.

### 7. [Kızılcık Şerbeti](/watch/tv/213194-kzlck-serbeti) (2022)

**Series · TMDB rating 6.7/10**

Free-spirited Doga falls for Fatih, but his family is deeply conservative and hers is modern, and neither side backs down.

### 8. [Eşref Rüya](/watch/tv/283123-esref-ruya) (2025)

**Series · TMDB rating 7.1/10**

A reckless rich man, Esref, is captivated by Nisan, a young musician.

## Crime and Action

### 9. [Family Secrets](/watch/tv/133490-family-secrets) (2021)

**Series · TMDB rating 8.7/10**

A rule-bending defence lawyer and a by-the-book prosecutor are forced to work together when his brother is accused of murder.

### 10. [Valley of the Wolves](/watch/tv/34587-valley-of-the-wolves) (2003)

**Series · TMDB rating 7.2/10**

One of Turkey's most influential series: undercover agent Polat Alemdar infiltrates Istanbul's most dangerous crime organisation.

### 11. [The Pit](/watch/tv/74823-the-pit) (2017)

**Series · TMDB rating 7.5/10**

When the Kocovar family risks losing control of their neighbourhood, the youngest son must come home to a place he never really escaped.

### 12. [The Shadow Team](/watch/tv/119806-the-shadow-team) (2021)

**Series · TMDB rating 7.4/10**

Elite intelligence agents run covert missions on one condition: the world must believe they are dead.
