Pakistani TV has had a strong run in late 2025 and 2026, with romances, family dramas and some darker psychological stories. Here are the newest Pakistani dramas people are watching right now, with a short note on what each one is about.

## Most Popular Right Now

### 1. [Meri Zindagi Hai Tu](/watch/tv/303911-meri-zindagi-hai-tu) (2025)

**Series**

Kamyar is charming but impulsive; Ayra is principled and kind. A sweet connection turns into a storm of emotion, deception and longing that tests their love.

### 2. [Sanwal Yaar Piya](/watch/tv/300202-sanwal-yaar-piya) (2025)

**Series**

Three lives, Sanwal, Aliyaar and Piya, collide in a story of love, fate, loyalty and sacrifice, with a complicated past hanging over all of them.

### 3. [Aik Mohabbat Aur](/watch/tv/321292-aik-mohabbat-aur) (2026)

**Series**

ACP Khushbakht moves back to her parents' home after a failed marriage and has given up on love. Then a case brings her together with Haroon, an idealistic journalist.

### 4. [Bas Tera Saath Ho](/watch/tv/320646-bas-tera-saath-ho) (2026)

**Series**

After losing his parents in an accident, young Anas ends up in a home that gives him a roof but no warmth. A story of grief and resilience.

### 5. [Zanjeerain](/watch/tv/321183-zanjeerain) (2026)

**Series**

In a beautiful mountain village, Rabiya runs a roadside bakery and slowly gets pulled into a conspiracy inside the local clan. Love, greed and tradition collide.

## New and Worth Starting

### 6. [Mitti De Baway](/watch/tv/336288-mitti-de-baway) (2026)

**Series**

A Punjab-set love story: Bano, from a respected family in Wazirabad, falls for Baala, a dhol player, but family honour stands between them.

### 7. [Muamma](/watch/tv/309105-muamma) (2025)

**Series**

Tenants carrying their own trauma move into an imposing old house owned by Jahan Ara, and the calm home slowly turns into a psychological puzzle.

### 8. [Case No. 9](/watch/tv/301468-case-no-9) (2025)

**Series**

A woman refuses to bow to her powerful boss, and her stand turns into a fight for dignity.

### 9. [Kafeel](/watch/tv/308362-kafeel) (2025)

**Series**

Zeba begins to suspect that the husband she trusted is not who he seems, as his behaviour changes and family pressure grows.

### 10. [Circle Story](/watch/tv/338286-circle-story) (2026)

**Series**

An anthology of 16 stories about people trapped in their own circles: of relationships, poverty, power or ambition.

### 11. [Dar-E-Nijaat](/watch/tv/328722-dar-e-nijaat) (2026)

**Series**

A drama about love, loss and the courage it takes to forgive.
