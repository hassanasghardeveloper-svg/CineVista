K-dramas keep getting more popular, and 2026 has some of the highest-rated new shows in years. Here are the best new Korean dramas of 2026, grouped by mood.

## Romance

### 1. [Love in Disguise](/watch/tv/303745-love-in-disguise) (2026)

**Series · TMDB rating 8.7/10**

Kang Jae-hee goes undercover as a male secretary to watch Yoon Yi-jun, the heir suspected of a murder. The closer they get to the truth, the closer they get to each other.

### 2. [Perfect Crown](/watch/tv/278573-perfect-crown) (2026)

**Series · TMDB rating 9.0/10**

In a modern Korea that still has a monarchy, a chaebol heiress who has everything except status makes a contract marriage with a lonely prince who has nothing but his title.

### 3. [Take Charge of My Heart](/watch/tv/289423-take-charge-of-my-heart) (2026)

**Series · TMDB rating 8.9/10**

A corporate heir with an artificial heart meets a woman who is literally electric, and the deal they make gets his heart beating in more ways than one.

### 4. [Between Steps](/watch/tv/307106-between-steps) (2026)

**Series · TMDB rating 9.1/10**

Minjae, a former swimmer who lost a leg to illness, has built a quiet life as a PhD student until Yujin turns it upside down in their robotics lab.

### 5. [Our Sticky Love](/watch/tv/291496-our-sticky-love) (2026)

**Series · TMDB rating 8.7/10**

An ambitious prosecutor loses her memory and moves in with a boxing coach who says he is her boyfriend.

### 6. [My Royal Nemesis](/watch/tv/303143-my-royal-nemesis) (2026)

**Series · TMDB rating 8.8/10**

A villainess from the Joseon era, fated to die, wakes up in modern Seoul, where a ruthless heir may be her only chance to change her fate.

## Action, Medical and Legal

**Series · TMDB rating 9.5/10**

### 8. [Teach You a Lesson](/watch/tv/276161-teach-you-a-lesson) (2026)

### 9. [Agent Kim Reactivated](/watch/tv/296206-agent-kim-reactivated) (2026)

**Series · TMDB rating 8.5/10**

When his daughter goes missing, a quiet dad brings his old black-ops skills back out to find her.

### 10. [Beyond the Bar](/watch/tv/281004-beyond-the-bar) (2025)

**Series · TMDB rating 8.1/10**

A rookie lawyer with a strong sense of justice joins a top firm under a cold, demanding mentor.

### 11. [Tomb Raider King](/watch/tv/297826-tomb-raider-king) (2026)

**Series · TMDB rating 8.1/10**

Mysterious tombs full of powerful relics appear around the world overnight, and a betrayed raider gets a second chance.
